## New cheap falsification: WFIM

The implemented map multiplies each word by the product of its letter scales: `s(w)=product(s(letter))`. For concatenation, `s(uv)=s(u)s(v)`. Thus for all nonzero scales:

`S^-1((Sx) * (Sy)) = x * y`.

This is a direct algebraic identity, not an underpowered null result. The audit's 200 finite numerical comparisons agree to maximum absolute error `3.552713678800501e-15`. Changing those parameters cannot change **this product**. A representation that exposes scaled coordinates to a decoder may be a different test of the original broader question; it must be stated explicitly and cannot inherit a product-expressivity claim. [Proof/check receipt](/Volumes/PRO-BLADE/World-Series/publication_audit/20260924/receipts/wfim_transport.json).

# INVALID OR UNSUPPORTED CLAIMS

The current shared research draft is generally careful about its scope. The following claims would exceed verified evidence, and some stale local documents still imply incorrect status:

1. **“No manuscript,” “synthetic data only,” “missing Celo2,” and “Ultron policies all tie.”** `world-series/docs/PAPER_AUDIT.md` still contains these older statements. A shared draft, UCI digit study, official Celo2 comparator and discriminating cap24 router result now exist. The old document should be preserved as history and current views reconciled under C00.
2. **A universal optimizer or superior MALIS result.** The primary digit AUC favors strong fixed controls. Step matching is not equal total compute. A favorable endpoint/ablation cannot replace the original objective.
3. **Full Q-APEN memory/lifecycle success.** Original memory is write-only; the new causal wrapper is a different bounded mechanism. Immediate spawn/activation is not the specified delayed-utility, subsequent-block-validated lifecycle.
4. **New quotient efficiency or Olympiad reasoning.** Valid small paths and projective identities do not establish discovery usefulness. The tested fixed-goal quotient produces no solve-rate advantage.
5. **Learned WCODE/CWLNN encoders.** WCODE uses fixed structural norms; CWLNN trains ridge readouts on fixed features. These cannot substantiate full trained relational or universal-backbone claims.
6. **A new WFIM algebra or useful transported product.** Foundations are classical/isomorphic, and current per-letter transport cancels. Finite exact arithmetic does not establish unknown infinite-tail bounds.
7. **WFT transfer or FFT-like scaling.** Each size is refit; full-basis reconstruction and true-operator oracle results are mathematical sanity checks, not learned usefulness or fast universal transforms.
8. **Beneficial verified physics/general PDE discovery.** WPINN's main candidate is weak-only oscillator fitting. Forcing zero damping is a deliberately wrong control, not the beneficial mechanism.
9. **Independent Ultron evaluation, general autonomy or ASI.** The actual result is nearest-neighbor selection among finite-DSL rankers with a local verifier. An evidence DAG or interface does not make evaluation independent.
10. **Publication readiness from receipt labels.** 159 SUCCEEDED run manifests coexist with three FAILED, one INTERRUPTED and one UNSUPPORTED. SUCCEEDED means execution, and some successful campaign runs contain scientifically failed trajectories.
