This queue closes the nine existing questions. It does not authorize new projects, redefine the original success criteria or turn development data into confirmation data. The audit and cheap WFIM falsification are executed; subsequent research work below is specified, not claimed complete.

All paths below are relative to `/Volumes/PRO-BLADE/World-Series/world-series/`. Proposed new files are explicitly marked **new**. Keep original runs/configurations intact. One numerical worker; CPU reference first. Compute figures are **planning ceilings, not measured estimates**. Each experiment gets a 30-minute maximum profiling job before its full allocation; if projected work exceeds its ceiling, stop and record a resource decision. Do not replace required evaluations with smaller demonstrations and call the question closed.

For every empirical gate: define one primary estimand, independent unit, practical effect margin, failure penalty/handling, hypothesis family, power calculation, tuning budget and stopping rule before seeing fresh outcomes. A positive closure requires a corrected confidence bound above the frozen meaningful-improvement margin at the declared cost. A negative closure requires enough precision to rule out that margin, or an appropriate structural falsification. Otherwise close as INCONCLUSIVE. No repeated tuning until a desired answer appears. Allow at most one documented development revision and one frozen final evaluation per gate; additional research requires an explicit new scientific decision.
