# WFIM bounded per-word coordinate mechanism

Status: IMPLEMENTED; downstream benefit NOT_RUN.

`src/world_series/wfim/coordinates.py` realizes the original positive diagonal per-word proposal on a frozen vocabulary of all words up to degree N. The empty-word scale is exactly one. Other scales are exp(b tanh(theta_w)), hence lie between exp(-b) and exp(b); the coordinate condition number is at most exp(2b). Vocabulary and concatenation-pair allocation limits are checked before tensor construction. Products accumulate only degree-retained concatenations through a fixed index map.

For a retained word w, the transported coefficient is the sum over uv=w of x_u y_v s_u s_v / s_w. Unlike multiplicative per-letter scales, independent per-word scales generally change these coefficients. Transport preserves associativity and the identity by conjugation. Degree truncation remains compatible because transport preserves degree and the discarded-degree subspace is an ideal. Numerical tests check round trips, nontrivial gradients, unit, associativity, bounded conditioning and a noncancelling product witness. This implementation uses floating-point tensors; it does not label these numerical tests as exact rational proofs.

The changed product is still isomorphic to concatenation. In particular, for repeated multiplication of one-hot letter inputs the final coefficient on word w is product_i s_(w_i) / s_w. For fixed scales this is a nonzero diagonal reweighting of the same word features. An unrestricted linear decoder can absorb that reweighting into its columns, so representational expressivity alone is not an advantage over the same algebra with identity coordinates. Any measured gain must arise from the declared finite optimization, regularization, decoder or storage regime. This is a reason for matched controls, not a reason to discard the original coordinate experiment.

Required next work: train the coordinate-aware downstream model against an identical identity-coordinate decoder, plus vector, bilinear and binding controls with explicit total storage accounting. Hold out order-sensitive templates and lengths, include an order-insensitive control, measure norm growth and full product costs, and preserve negative results. Tensor payload and canonical vocabulary sizes are reported separately from Python overhead, optimizer states and serialized checkpoint container sizes; total-byte matching must count all required components.
